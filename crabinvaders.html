Your job is to keep the crabs in the ocean.

They don't want to stay in the ocean, but it's good for them, so you have to help put them back.
You have a seagull chief of staff to help you.
Hit SPACE to throw a flashlight at the crabs to show your CoS where to go.
It's space invaders, but with crabs.
This game is adapted from yojoecool's SpaceInvader program.
