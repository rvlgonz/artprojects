Hello, world!

Daniel started his journey towards sustainability policy as a environmental studies major at Miami University (Ohio).
He continued on to get his Masters of Sustainability Policy at Indiana University.
While at IU, he carried out research under the supervision of future Deputy Director of the EPA, Janet McCabe.

What is The Policy Database?

The Policy Database started with the recognition that many non-profits and local governments want to base their decisions on rigorous research and data, but are under-resourced to develop policy papers efficiently.
Daniel wants to bring his expertise in policy to help make these resources available and accessible to all who could use them.
Go to the database

How to participate:

Use: All policy papers on the database are available for use and modification with citation (The Policy Database, [author],[year]).
Submissions, requests, and suggestions: If you would like to submit a policy paper for inclusion, have a paper request, or want to help improve the site, please use the contact form. Daniel will reach out to follow-up.
Contributors: If you would like to become a regular contributor or editor, please contact
